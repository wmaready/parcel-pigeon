Currently, the Service Guarantee is in effect for select Air and International services but is otherwise suspended. We remain
committed to providing the excellent service that customers have come to expect. For further details on the Service
Guarantee, visit UPS Service Guarantee | UPS - United States.
The most up-to-date transit times are available at ups.com/ctc at the time of shipping.
UPS Air and International Air
Scheduled delivery times for Air and International Air* packages scheduled for delivery Nov. 27, 2026 – Jan. 2, 2027 will be
extended as follows: UPS Next Day Air® Early and UPS Worldwide Express Plus® services will be extended by 90 minutes,
UPS Next Day Air®, UPS Worldwide Express®, and UPS 2nd Day Air® A.M. services to commercial locations with a typical 10:30
a.m. or 12:00 p.m. delivery time will be extended to 3:00 p.m. UPS Next Day Air® and UPS Worldwide Express® services to
residential locations and all other time definite Air and International Air services will be extended to end-of-day.
A limited number of UPS 2nd Day Air® and UPS 3 Day Select® packages tendered on the following dates may require an
additional day in transit:
• UPS 2nd Day Air® tendered on: Dec. 14, Dec. 15, Dec. 16 or Dec. 21
• UPS 3 Day Select® tendered on: Dec. 14 or Dec. 15
The most up-to-date transit time details will be available at ups.com/ctc at the time of shipping.
UPS® Ground and UPS® Standard
From Nov. 16, 2026 – Jan. 8, 2027, a limited number of UPS® Ground and UPS® Standard packages will require additional time
in transit. The most up-to-date transit time details will be available at ups.com/ctc at the time of shipping.
Time-in-transit notes
On UPS holidays — Nov. 26, Dec. 25, and Jan. 1 — there is no movement of any packages tendered to UPS, regardless of the
date of tender.
