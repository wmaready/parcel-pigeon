DHL Group continues its growth trajectory in the third quarter and
significantly increases revenue and earnings
 Group revenue increased 12.6 percent to EUR 22.7 billion
(Q3 2025: EUR 20.1 billion)
 Operating profit (EBIT) rose 16.2 percent to EUR 1.7 billion
(Q3 2025: EUR 1.5 billion)
 Free cash flow (excluding M&A) reached EUR 2.7 billion after the first nine months
of the year (9M 2025: EUR 2.3 billion)
 Following the guidance increase in October, DHL Group expects operating profit of
more than EUR 7.0 billion and free cash flow (excluding M&A) of more than
EUR 3.2 billion for 2026
 CEO Tobias Meyer: “We successfully continued our growth trajectory in the third
quarter, delivering a significant increase in revenue and earnings. Our strong
operational performance enabled us to raise our full-year earnings guidance for the
second time this year.”
Bonn, November 5, 2026: DHL Group continued its strong business performance in the third quarter
of 2026. Group revenue increased 12.6 percent year-over-year to EUR 22.7 billion (Q3 2025: EUR 20.1
billion). Operating profit (EBIT) rose 16.2 percent to EUR 1.7 billion (Q3 2025: EUR 1.5 billion). The
EBIT margin improved to 7.6 percent (Q3 2025: 7.3 percent). Based on the continued positive earnings
momentum, the Group raised its full-year 2026 guidance to an operating profit of more than EUR 7.0
billion (previously: more than EUR 6.5 billion) on October 16 and announced its preliminary results.
The positive business performance was driven by growth across a wide range of regions and sectors,
particularly in DHL Express, DHL Global Forwarding, and DHL Supply Chain. DHL Express recorded
very strong earnings growth. With its targeted offering for time-critical heavy-weight shipments for
industrial customers, the division significantly increased transported weight, improving utilization of its
global network. Disciplined yield and capacity management, together with structural cost
improvements implemented over the past two years, further supported earnings performance.
Temporary capacity constraints in the international air freight market generated additional demand,
which DHL Express served through its own global network.
Press release
“We successfully continued our growth trajectory in the third quarter, delivering a significant increase in
revenue and earnings. Our strong operational performance enabled us to raise our full-year earnings
guidance for the second time this year. We remain focused on providing our customers with high service
quality and sufficient capacity. Even in challenging conditions, we help them access markets and unlock
new growth opportunities. That is why we are stepping up investments in our network and in new
logistics capabilities needed to tap into the markets of the future.”
Tobias Meyer, CEO DHL Group
Positive business performance supports cash flow
The strong operational performance is also reflected in cash flow. Free cash flow (excluding M&A)
reached EUR 901 million in the third quarter (Q3 2025: EUR 1.2 billion) – including net cash outflows
of EUR 256 million related to the U.S. tariffs (IEEPA). In the first nine months, DHL Group generated
free cash flow (excluding M&A) of EUR 2.7 billion (9M 2025: EUR 2.3 billion). Consolidated net profit
after non-controlling interests amounted to EUR 907 million in the third quarter (Q3 2025: EUR 840
million). Basic earnings per share reached EUR 0.82, up 9.6 percent from EUR 0.75 in the third quarter
of 2025.
Targeted investments address changing customer needs
Despite geopolitical uncertainty, global trade continues to grow, even as trade flows are shifting. In this
environment, customers benefit from DHL Group’s global presence and operational flexibility. In line
with Strategy 2030, the Group is further developing its service offering to address evolving customer
requirements and additional logistics needs. Through its strategic growth initiatives, DHL Group
focuses on regions and sectors where it expects particularly dynamic growth in customer demand.
These include Life Sciences & Healthcare, New Energy, E-commerce, and Data Center Logistics.
Capital expenditures on acquired assets (capex) amounted to EUR 897 million in the third quarter.
After the first nine months, capex totaled EUR 2.2 billion, up EUR 532 million compared with the prior-
year period. In Data Center Logistics, for example, DHL Supply Chain is investing in additional
warehouse locations in North America while also expanding its capacity in the Asia Pacific region. DHL
Group is combining expertise across multiple divisions to offer integrated end-to-end solutions for
data centers. In addition, the Group continues to invest in digitalization, automation, and the
capabilities of its existing network.
Press release
Guidance raised for the second time
Given the continued positive earnings momentum, particularly at DHL Express, DHL Global Forwarding,
and DHL Supply Chain, as well as initial signs of robust seasonal developments in the fourth quarter, DHL
Group raised its guidance for fiscal year 2026 for the second time on October 16.
The Group now expects operating profit (EBIT) of more than EUR 7.0 billion (previously: more than EUR
6.5 billion). EBIT for the DHL divisions is forecast at more than EUR 6.4 billion (previously: more than
EUR 5.9 billion). Expectations for Post & Parcel Germany, with EBIT of more than EUR 900 million, and
for Group Functions, at around EUR -400 million, remain unchanged.
Guidance for free cash flow (excluding M&A) was raised to more than EUR 3.2 billion (previously: around
EUR 3.0 billion). To capture additional growth opportunities, the Group plans capital expenditures on
acquired assets (capex) of around EUR 3.6 billion in 2026 (previously: EUR 3.0 billion to EUR 3.3 billion).
In March 2027, DHL Group will review its existing medium-term guidance of operating profit of more
than EUR 7.0 billion in a normalized economic environment.
“Our continued strong earnings and cash flow generation enable us to invest selectively in further growth.
We maintain a disciplined approach to capital allocation, strengthening the capabilities and
efficiency of our network. ”
Melanie Kreis, CFO DHL Group
DHL Express: Improved network utilization driven by higher shipment weight

EBIT (in EUR million) 692 1,038 +50.0 (1)
EBIT margin (in percent) 11.8 14.8 +3.0 (2)
(1) in percent (2) in percentage points
DHL Express continued its strong business performance in the third quarter. With its offering for time-
critical heavy-weight shipments for industrial customers, the division increased the total weight of
shipments transported, resulting in better utilization of its network. Together with ongoing cost and
yield management measures, this contributed to significant earnings growth. Third-quarter earnings
also included a positive effect of around EUR 100 million from temporary capacity constraints in the
international air freight market.

DHL Global Forwarding significantly increased revenue and operating profit compared with the prior-
year quarter. Air freight volumes grew particularly strongly on trade lanes originating in Asia. Ocean
freight growth was primarily driven by higher volumes from Asia and Europe. With its global network
and local market expertise, the division helped customers adapt transport routes to changing trade
flows and maintain reliable supply chains.

DHL Supply Chain continued its growth trajectory and recorded revenue growth across all regions. The
Americas business made a particularly important contribution, including in the Technology and
Consumer sectors. Strong new contract wins, particularly in Retail including e-fulfillment solutions,
and Technology, provide a solid basis for future growth. The division continues to invest in automation,
digitalization, and additional capacity to support its customers with modern logistics processes.

DHL eCommerce generated organic revenue growth of around 6 percent. Reported revenue
development was affected by portfolio changes, particularly deconsolidation in the United Kingdom
and Portugal. The decline in reported EBIT versus the prior-year figure was primarily due to positive
one-off effects of EUR 123 million in the third quarter of 2025. The division continues to integrate
recent acquisitions while continuously optimizing its networks and processes.

The domestic and international parcel business continued to support Post & Parcel Germany’s
performance. Parcel volume development was impacted, among other factors, by the abolition of the
de minimis exemption in the European Union as of July 1 this year. At the same time, the structural
decline in mail volumes continued. Higher costs for personnel, energy, and transportation put
additional pressure on operating profit.

DHL Group is the world’s leading logistics company. The Group connects people and markets and is an
enabler of global trade. It aspires to be the first choice for customers, employees, investors and green
logistics worldwide. To this end, DHL Group is focusing on accelerating sustainable growth in its
profitable core logistics businesses and Group growth initiatives. The Group contributes to the world
through sustainable business practices, corporate citizenship, and environmental activities. By the year
2050, DHL Group aims to achieve net-zero emissions logistics.
DHL Group is home to two strong brands: DHL offers a comprehensive range of parcel, express, freight
transport, and supply chain management services as well as E-commerce logistics solutions. Deutsche
Post is the largest postal service provider in Europe and the market leader in the German mail
market. DHL Group employs approximately 584,000 people in over 220 countries and territories
worldwide. The Group generated revenue of approximately EUR 82.9 billion in 2025.
