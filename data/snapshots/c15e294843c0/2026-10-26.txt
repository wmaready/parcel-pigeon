Spee-Dee Delivery is a regional delivery and logistics company that has served the
Upper Midwest since 1978. Known for our reliable, cost-effective parcel delivery
services, Spee-Dee Delivery operates within ten states including Illinois, Iowa, Kansas,
Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, South Dakota, and Wisconsin.
Our company focuses on providing next-day and second-day delivery to customers of all
sizes, with an emphasis on customer service, competitive rates, and regional expertise.
Spee-Dee LTL offers fast, cost-effective regional freight shipping for customers across
the Upper Midwest. With reliable transit times, real-time tracking, and personalized
customer service, Spee-Dee ensures efficient delivery of LTL shipments. Our extensive
regional network enables next-day service to many areas, making us a trusted partner for
customers seeking dependable, affordable logistics solutions with the same trustworthy
service that defines our parcel operations.
Spee-Dee Logistics further enhances our company’s capabilities by offering customized
warehousing, fulfillment, and distribution solutions. Designed to streamline supply
chains, Spee-Dee Logistics helps businesses store, manage, and ship inventory with
precision and speed. Spee-Dee Logistics supports inventory management, order picking,
packing, and final-mile delivery, all under one roof. This integrated approach reduces
costs, improves efficiency, and offers scalability for growing businesses.
Spee-Dee Delivery’s new Transportation Management System, Spee-Dee shipit powered
by Dispatch Science, streamlines shipping with real-time tracking, easy label creation,
and seamless integration. Designed for efficiency, it simplifies logistics for businesses of
all sizes — enhancing accuracy, visibility, and reporting throughout the delivery process.
A smarter way to ship with Spee-Dee.
Together, Spee-Dee Delivery, Spee-Dee LTL, and Spee-Dee Logistics form a comprehensive
logistics network tailored to the unique needs of regional customers. Our services are
rooted in a deep understanding of the Midwest market, offering an alternative to national
carriers with more localized, responsive service. Whether delivering a single package,
a pallet of freight, or managing end-to-end logistics, Spee-Dee provides consistent,
dependable solutions that keep Midwest commerce moving.
