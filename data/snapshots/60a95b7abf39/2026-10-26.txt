How to calculate the Pickup Area Surcharge
1. Determine the pickup ZIP code for your pickup address.
2. Select the interactive links above for lists of the ZIP codes where the
pickup area surcharge applies. The ZIP codes are listed sequentially .
3. If your pickup ZIP code is listed, the pickup area surcharge applies
to your pickup. For surcharge amounts, see the FedEx Service Guide.
