FEDEX INTERNATIONAL CONTROLLED EXPORT (FICE)
TERMS AND CONDITIONS
Effective February 12, 2026
FedEx International Controlled Export (FICE) is a service option for FedEx International Priority, FedEx
International Priority Express, FedEx International Priority Freight, and FedEx International Priority
DirectDistribution services allowing shippers to tender U.S. export shipments requiring pre-export
regulatory clearances. Unless the FICE service option is used, U.S. export shipments requiring pre-export
regulatory clearances are prohibited. Shipments tendered using the FICE service option are governed by
the terms and conditions in the current FedEx Service Guide, the terms and conditions of the
automation system used to prepare this shipping transaction, as well as these additional terms and
conditions of use (this "Agreement"). Please read this information carefully. If you agree to be bound by
this Agreement, you must acknowledge your agreement below on behalf of yourself and the entity, if
any, who you are employed by or represent. Once acknowledged, this document will constitute a legal
agreement between Federal Express Corporation ("FedEx"), you, and the legal entity you are employed
by or represent (collectively referred to as "Shipper" or "You"). This Agreement will govern all use of the
FICE service option. Please print out a copy of this Agreement and retain it with your files for future
reference.
SECTION 1. AVAILABILITY AND USE
The FICE service option is available only to U.S.- and Puerto Rico-based shippers with an active FedEx
account number. A FICE-enabled FedEx Ship Manager® device and/or FedEx Ship Manager® at
fedex.com must be used to create air waybills for FICE shipments. FICE customers may utilize FedEx’s
customs bond when FedEx is acting as the bonded carrier for the FICE shipments.
SECTION 2. ADDITIONAL SHIPPER RESPONSIBILITIES
Shipper agrees to comply with the following requirements with respect to every shipment for which the
FICE service option is selected:
1. Identify each FICE shipment by selecting the FICE option and the type of controlled export at time
of shipment.
2. Provide complete and accurate data for Electronic Export Information (EEI) when FedEx is filing the
EEI on your behalf.
3. Provide FedEx with all paperwork required for export and import clearance (e.g., Commercial
Invoice, license, copy of EEI filing). If you filed the EEI, you must provide FedEx with the Internal
Transaction Number (ITN) received from the Automated Export System (AES). If an EEI is not required to
be filed, you must provide the proper Foreign Trade Regulations (FTR) exemption that applies. If the
shipment is shipped under a DEA permit or State Department License, you cannot use an FTR exemption
as a general matter. You must ensure the filing of an EEI where required and provide the ITN once filed.
There are cases where an exemption under the FTR applies even for shipments requiring pre-export
regulatory clearances and an EEI filing is not required. It is your responsibility to review the relevant
regulations and government guidelines to determine when an EEI is required and ensure accuracy of
information in any EEI filings that are made.
4. When you are shipping licensable and non-licensable commodities to the same recipient on the
same day, only licensable commodities may be tendered as part of the FICE shipment and any non-
licensable commodities must be tendered to FedEx as a separate shipment on a separate air waybill.
5. Any license or permit decrementation is your responsibility. If shipment information is transmitted
to FedEx, but the shipment is not subsequently tendered to FedEx on the same shipping day, you may
need to ensure the relevant licenses/permits are decremented with a correct export date.
The Shipper, Consignee, or Third Party selected to pay the transportation charges will pay the $50.00
USD/shipment FedEx Handling Fee associated with this service option.
SECTION 3. TERMINATION
Shipper may cease using the FICE service option at any time. FedEx may discontinue, suspend, or modify
FICE service at any time, for any reason, with or without notice. In addition, FedEx may modify or
terminate this Agreement at any time, without notice.
SECTION 4. IMPROPER USE
In the event that Shipper improperly declares or tenders any shipment using the FICE service option, or
U.S. Customs and Border Protection disallows export of a FICE shipment, FedEx may at its sole
discretion consider the shipment undeliverable and the Undeliverable Shipments provisions in the FedEx
Service Guide will govern return, storage or disposal of the shipment.
