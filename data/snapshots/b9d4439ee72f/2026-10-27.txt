The OnTrac Fuel Surcharge is index-based and adjusted weekly. The Fuel Surcharge is assessed on the
net
package Rate plus all additional fees and Surcharges, as applicable.
The Fuel Surcharge will be based on the West Coast (PADD5) Average On-Highway Diesel Fuel
Price as most
recently
released by the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), that is one (1) week prior to the adjustment, rounded to the nearest cent. Changes to Fuel Surcharges will be effective on the following Monday of an applicable calendar week. Fuel Surcharge percentages and thresholds are subject to change without prior notice. For example, the Fuel Surcharge for the week of February 10, 2025, will be based on the National U.S. Average On-Highway Diesel Fuel Price released by EIA the week of February 3, 2025. If the information released by the EIA is delayed, OnTrac may use applicable fuel pricing information from an earlier calendar week.
