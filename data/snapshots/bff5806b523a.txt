UPS Ground Saver®
UPS Ground Saver (formerly UPS SurePost®) is a lower-cost shipping option designed for less urgent deliveries. Talk to your sales rep or book a consultation to learn more and start saving.
Reliable, Affordable Delivery
Customers with a UPS contract can utilize Ground Saver, best for businesses shipping non‑urgent packages who want a cost‑saving alternative to UPS Ground.
FAQ
UPS Ground Saver transit times are typically comparable to UPS Ground, plus approximately one to two additional business days. Actual delivery time depends on factors such as origin, destination and when the shipment is tendered to UPS.
Delivery days generally occur Monday through Saturday.
The main difference between UPS Ground Saver and UPS Ground is speed and cost.
- UPS Ground Saver is a cost‑saving option designed for non‑urgent shipments and may involve USPS for final delivery.
- UPS Ground offers faster delivery times with end‑to‑end handling by UPS.
Businesses that prioritize lower shipping costs over delivery speed may choose UPS Ground Saver, while time‑sensitive shipments are better suited for UPS Ground.
UPS Ground Saver Details
UPS Ground Saver Features
- Delivery
- Packages delivered by UPS and the USPS.
- Delivery Days
- Monday - Saturday
- Origin and Destination
- UPS Ground Saver service is currently available for packages picked up within the 48 contiguous United States for delivery to residential addresses in the 48 contiguous United States, Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, U.S. Territories and to U.S. Post Office Boxes™ and APO/FPO/DPO addresses. UPS Ground Saver service is not available for international packages.
- Manifesting
- Package level detail (PLD) provided through UPS WorldShip®, UPS CampusShip®, UPS.com shipping, UPS Shipping API, UPSLink Vendor, and many third party shipping systems.
- Tracking
- Package-level tracking available via UPS tracking number.
- Loss or Damage Coverage
- Included coverage up to $50 per package, subject to applicable terms and conditions.
