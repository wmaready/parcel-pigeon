1. Our Service
Table of Contents 3Amazon Shipping Service Guide
Amazon Shipping offers a fast, affordable shipping service with delivery you can count on.
We operate 7 days a week, available to eligible shippers in the contiguous United States.
Once onboarded, you can use Amazon Shipping to fulfill customer orders across your
sales channels, including your e-commerce website, Amazon. com, and other
marketplaces.
You can purchase shipping labels directly through Shipper Central, the Buy Shipping
feature in Seller Central, or through a variety of other channels including approved
ecommerce solutions. You can also integrate directly into your own systems using the
Amazon Shipping API. Please note: not all features and capabilities of the Amazon
Shipping service described in this Service Guide are available when purchasing labels
outside of Shipper Central or the Amazon Shipping API. Similarly, merchant rating
protection features available for shipping labels purchased through Buy Shipping may
not be available for labels purchased through other channels, including Shipper
Central.
Once your shipments are ready, schedule a time for us to pick them up or simply take
them to one of our available drop-off locations. This Service Guide provides an
overview of the services we offer and the requirements for shipping with us. It
supplements the Amazon Shipping Terms and Conditions, which apply to you and all
packages you ship with us.
2. Customer Support
Table of Contents 4Amazon Shipping Service Guide
Our dedicated Shipper Support team is available through Shipper Central to provide
guidance on a range of topics, including pick-up scheduling, billing queries, claims
issues and support with shipment tracking. You can contact support by phone and chat
from 5 am to 11 pm Pacific Time and by email 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, each
through the “contact us” page on your Shipper Central account. If you do not have a
Shipper Central account, you can contact the authorized ecommerce solution where
you purchased your labels for support.
3. Shipping Fees and List Rates
Table of Contents 5Amazon Shipping Service Guide
Overview
Your Shipping Fees are your total shipping costs consist of two components: the Base Shipping Fee and anyAdditional Fees.
These may be different from those listed in this Service Guide if you have negotiated custom pricing (which will be available as
a Custom Price Proposal in Shipper Central) or are purchasing your shipping labels outsideof Shipper Central (if different, the
rates listed on that channel will apply).
We calculate the Base Shipping Fee by determining the Billable Weight for each package and then applying the
corresponding List Rates:
•The Billable Weight is the greater of (i) actual weight and (ii) dimensional weight, which is calculated based
on actual package volume and your dimensional divisor. Note: Billable Weight is always rounded up to the
nearest pound.
•The List Rates are the applicable minimum fees for each Shipment and are calculated by distance (the Zone) and
the Billable Weight. See page 6 for our current List Rates and Zones.
Additional Fees are fees, including surcharges or special handling fees, that apply in specific circumstances and are in
addition to the Base Shipping Fee. See Section 4 for a list of Additional Fees that may apply.
Zones are based on the miles from origin zip code to destination zip code (except as otherwise communicated by us):
Zone 6: 1,001–1,400 miles
Zone 7: 1,401–1,800 miles
Zone 8: 1,801 miles and over
Zone 2: 0 –150 miles
Zone 3: 151–300 miles
Zone 4: 301–600 miles
Zone 5: 601–1,000 miles
Note: All Zones exclude shipments to or from AK, HI, PR, and US Territories. Amazon Shipping does not currently deliverto any
location outside of the contiguous United States.

2.Preparation
Packaging requirements
Your shipments must be packaged properly for safe transport and we may reject packages which do not meet our
requirements. Each shipment must be:
•Fully encased in packaging (corrugated cardboard or padded envelope) that is strong enough to avoid damageto other
shipments or injury to persons and must not have overwrap.
•No more than 50 pounds, with a maximum length of 59 inches, height of 33 inches, width of 33 inches, and a girth of130
inches. Length is defined as the longest side of the parcel and girth is defined as Girth = Length + 2 (Width + Height).If received
into our network, packages will be subject to the Non-Standard, Additional Handling, Large Package, andExtra Heavy Package
Fees.
•Sealed using shipping tape. String, rope, cellophane tape, or masking tape may not be used to secure the outer packaging.
•Free from plastic strapping or banding on the outer packaging, including strapping or banding together separate items or
packages, which must be shipped as separate Shipments.
•Free from shiny or reflective packaging that may interfere with scanning.
•Filled completely with a suitable filler or cushioning material to ensure the shipment stays intact during transit.
•If it contains sharp objects, heavy cardboard should be applied around all sharp edges and points to ensure theydo not
pierce packaging.
•If it contains liquids, they should be wrapped and sealed to prevent leakage and surrounded with absorbentmaterial to
avoid breakage, which requires, at minimum, packaging such Shipments in both a leak-proof inner liner and a leak-proof
outer box.
•Previously used packaging is acceptable, provided that it is undamaged and all labels or markings related to the prior
shipment have been removed.
Labeling requirements
You must label your shipments in accordance with our requirements and applicable law. We may re-issue all or a portion ofthe
shipping label affixed to the Shipment. Our requirements include:
•Shipping label stock, onto which each shipping label that you purchase is printed on, must be peel-off thermal labels thatare 4 inches
x 6 inches and do not contain any third-party markings. The details for each shipping label must be printed inblack ink on a plain
white background, entirely within the borders of the label. You may not modify or include additionalidentifiers on the shipping
label without our prior written approval.
•Place the shipping label, along with any other required labels and markings, on a single surface, preferably the largest
surface away from the edges. Remove any outdated labels from the packaging before applying a shipping label.
•When you place an order for one shipment, you can only purchase one shipping label. You may not use the same
Shipment ID or shipping label on multiple Shipments.
•Indicate fragile contents or Dangerous Goods, using appropriate labels or markings. Ensure you are complying withthe
specific requirements in the Dangerous Goods Shipping Guide.
Palletization requirements
You may be asked to palletize your Shipments if the shipment volume or mix is unsuitable for a loose load pick-up.If asked
to palletize, you must supply your own pallets and follow these requirements:
•Pallets must be in good condition and not exceed 48 inches in length; 77 inches in height (including the pallet height),or
2,000 pounds in gross weight (including pallet weight).
•Pallets must be shrink wrapped.
•Pallets may only contain packages with Amazon shipping labels.
•Pallets must be assembled with heavier items at the bottom and lighter items at the top to ensure stability and safety.
•Shipping labels and any required labels or markings must be applied to the center of the pallet, approximately one-
third of the way down from the top, and on the side that will be accessed by a pallet jack or forklift.
