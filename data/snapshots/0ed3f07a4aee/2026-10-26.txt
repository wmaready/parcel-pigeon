Postal Service Prepped To Deliver for 2026 Holiday Season
• Building upon 2025 peak season accomplishments
• Continued new vehicle deployment
• Increased daily processing capacity
• USPS remains the most affordable way to ship mail and packages
WASHINGTON — The Postal Service is ready to deliver the holidays for 2026. The organization is
continuing to build upon the successful delivery performance of the 2025 peak season.
Thanks to investments in new technology and new logistics planning and implementation by the
organization, the Postal Service significantly improved its delivery performance during last year’s holiday
season. In 2025, mail and packages were delivered, on average, in 2.5 days compared with 2.8 days
during the same period in 2024 (Nov. 15, 2024-Jan. 9, 2025).
Plans for holiday deliveries begin in January. Preparing so far in advance enables the Postal Service to
manage the influx of mail and packages it receives — volumes increase about 40 percent between Oct. 1
and Dec. 31 — for the holiday season
“The Postal Service performed very well during last year’s holiday season, and we are even better
prepared this year for the upcoming peak mailing and shipping season,” Postmaster General David
Steiner said. “We have been preparing all year for this peak season, and we have enough capacity to
handle whatever comes our way.”
Key accomplishments and areas of progress ahead of the 2026 peak holiday season include:
• Increasing daily processing capacity by deploying more than 600 package sorters: USPS
has expanded its daily package processing capacity by adding 673 state-of-the-art package
sorting machines over the past six years, including 164 installed this year alone. The machines
have automated scanning capabilities that ensure tracking visibility for our customers as
packages move through the postal system. These machines allow us to handle more and larger
parcels than the legacy machines and prepare the Postal Service for the expected surge in
volume for the holidays. Also, a new, next-generation package tracking and reporting platform
increases package visibility within the postal network.
• Hiring more than 10,000 seasonal employees: Within the last six years, a combination of
improved staffing, reduced supervisor vacancies and lowered overtime rates has resulted in a
more stabilized workforce. This has allowed the Postal Service to hire a little more than 10,000
seasonal employees — down from 40,000 just a few years ago.
• Launching next-generation package tracking and reporting platform: In 2025, USPS
launched its next-generation package tracking and reporting platform. The tracking is nearly four
times faster than the previous version, often providing updates within a minute of scanning. The
platform works seamlessly with other USPS systems to not only allow customers to see a more
accurate location of their packages using the tracking tool on usps.com or Informed Delivery, but
to also ensure packages are delivered to the correct address.
• Debuting new facilities: Since 2022, the Postal Service has opened a total of 182 sorting and
delivery centers (S&DCs), 19 regional processing and distribution centers (RPDCs) associated
with 68 local processing centers (LPCs), and four standalone regional transfer hubs (RTHs). Of
this total, in fiscal year 2026, USPS launched 31 S&DCs and 10 RPDCs working in tandem with
35 LPCs.
• Improving proactive, strategic planning in targeted markets: The organization recently
created a Chief Solutions and Strategy Office to look across its entire enterprise, centralize
network planning and identify ways to work more effectively and efficiently. Additionally, this
group is working more closely with USPS customers and taking a consultative approach to
matching customer needs with postal solutions.
• Deploying new postal delivery vehicles: Within the last three years, USPS has received and
deployed more than 45,000 new vehicles nationwide. This new fleet is a combination of both gas
and electric powered, commercial off the shelf (COTS), and purpose built, next generation
delivery vehicles (NGDVs). The modern fleet provides increased fuel economy and reduced
carbon emissions, while ensuring safe and reliable service. New vehicles will continue to be
deployed throughout the 2026 holiday season and into the next calendar year.
• Making the returns process easier: USPS offers a seamless experience for both customers
and merchants through an unmatched retail network and flexible returns options. Customers can
drop off returns packages at any of the organization’s more than 31,000 retail locations, use one
of its more than 3,000 self-service kiosks, or schedule a free carrier pickup to help make returns
stress-free any time of the year.
• Installing USPS Smart Lockers for a safe and flexible way to send and receive packages:
USPS has installed hundreds of smart lockers in cities throughout the country. The lockers
provide an alternative free, secure and convenient solution for customers and businesses to send
and receive packages when home or business delivery isn’t practical or preferred.
• Making products and services widely available for shipping gifts: The Postal Service has a
range of convenient and affordable options for customers to get their mail and packages where
they need to go during the holidays quickly and reliably. With $100 insurance and tracking
included on most shipments, customers can rely on USPS Ground Advantage (2-5 business
days) *, Priority Mail (2-3 business days) and Priority Mail Express (1-3 business days) during the
holidays.
* USPS Ground Advantage packages between the contiguous United States and the following locations will have a 10-day or more
service expectation: Alaska, Hawaii, American Samoa, Puerto Rico, United States Virgin Islands, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands
and the Freely Associated States. Days will vary due to the range of distances between locations. Customers can look up service
expectations on usps.com for mailings from one ZIP Code to another on a particular mailing date:
https://postcalc.usps.com/ServiceCommitments.
As in years past, the Postal Service will also prioritize morning parcel delivery, enhanced scanning
technology and extended retail and pickup delivery hours at popular Post Office locations . It will also
continue to offer the lowest postage rates in the industrialized world. Additionally, USPS delivers more
mail than any other postal system — serving more than 170 million addresses — and processes and
delivers nearly half of the world’s mail.

Please Note: The United States Postal Service is an independent federal establishment, mandated to be self-financing and to serve
every American community through the affordable, reliable and secure delivery of mail and packages to more than 170 million
addresses six and often seven days a week. Overseen by a bipartisan Board of Governors, the Postal Service is celebrating its 250th
year of service to customers amidst a network modernization plan aimed at restoring long-term financial sustainability, improving
service, and maintaining the organization as one of America’s most valued and trusted brands.
The Postal Service generally receives no tax dollars for operating expenses and relies on the sale of postage, products and services
to fund its operations.
