LTL Guidelines/Additional Charges
Per Pallet Maximums
-Cannot exceed 96’’ in height
-Cannot exceed 5,000 pounds. 5,000 pounds is limited to equipment available.
-If a lift gate is required a pallet cannot exceed 2,600 pounds and pallet cannot exceed 4’ x 7’
-Must be movable with a standard pallet jack
-Hazardous Materials limited to 500 pounds collectively (per day)
-Declared Value cannot exceed $5,000 per pallet (see charges below)
Non-Palletized Shipment Maximums
-Hazardous Materials limited to 500 pounds collectively (per day)
-Declared Value cannot exceed $5,000 per shipment (see charges below)
-150 pounds per piece (any piece over 100 pounds must have a second person for loading and unloading)
-Maximum length is 20’ / Maximum weight on a per foot charge is 500 pounds per foot of trailer space
Additional Charges
 Address Correction - $35.00 (flat fee per shipment)
 Reconsignment Fee – A change in the place of delivery $35.00 (flat fee per shipment)
 Scheduled Delivery – Appointment needed or a 2 hour or less window for delivery $45.00 (flat fee per shipment)
 Scheduled Pick-Up – Appointment needed or a 2 hour or less window for pick-up $45.00 (flat fee per shipment)
 COD – Collecting payment from customer for products $35.00 (charges based upon number of COD’s collected)
 Island Ferry Fee - $20.00 per hundred pounds or fraction thereof. ZIP Codes 54246 and 54850
 Declared Value – Shipments requiring a Declared Value greater than $100 must be clearly identified on the BOL. If no Declared Value is
purchased the maximum claim paid out will be $100 per pallet or per shipment. Charges are $1.30 for each additional $100 or fraction
thereof with a $3.90 minimum.
o Palletized – Maximum Declared Value is $5,000 per pallet
o Non-Palletized – Maximum Declared Value is $5,000 per shipment

Additional Charges per Pallet Position
If pallets exceed 48” in length or width the price is determined by the pallet position it utilizes in 2 foot increments.
Please contact an LTL Representative for an estimate.
 Delivery Area Surcharge “DAS” – applies to shipments with a destination ZIP Code that falls within the parameters
of our DAS ZIP Code list. DAS – $15.00 per pallet position or non-palletized piece, Maximum of $300.00
 Pallet Correction Fee - $20.00 per pallet plus actual shipment charges
 Lift Gate required – Needing a lift gate service at pick-up or delivery location $60.00 (per location, per position) Maximum of $300
 Hand Load – Driver needing to hand load shipment $60.00
 Hand Unload – Driver needing to hand unload shipment $60.00
 Residential Pick-Up $35.00 (if location does not have an entrance open to the public) Maximum of $280
 Residential Delivery $35.00 (if location does not have an entrance open to the public) Maximum of $280
 Forwarding Fee – When origin, destination, or third party is a transportation company $35.00
 Special Equipment Required – If facility requires a special size vehicle for pick-up or delivery $50.00
 Hazardous Materials – Limited to 500 pounds collectively (per day) $40.00
 Blind Shipment - $35.00 (charges based upon number of blind locations, per pallet position)
 Failed Pick-Up Attempt – $68.00 (when product has been scheduled for pick-up and it is not ready or released by the shipper for whatever
reason)
 Pallet Fulfillment - $6.00, Wrap Fulfillment - $9.00
** Applicable LTL fuel surcharge is added to the cost of the entire shipment **
