Index-based Package Fuel Surcharge
Effective November 17, 2025, our Index-based surcharge is as follows. The surcharge is based on the
National U.S. Average On-Highway Diesel Fuel Prices reported by the U.S. Department of Energy. The
surcharge is adjusted weekly – changes are effective Monday through Sunday, using the price from one
week prior.
The following table illustrates the surcharge:
National US Average On-Highway Diesel Fuel Price - $/gallon
For prices at or above $4.99, the fuel surcharge will equal 18.25% plus an additional 0.25% for each additional $0.09 in fuel price.
Fuel Surcharge is applied to all charges.
