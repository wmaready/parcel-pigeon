How to calculate the Delivery Area Surcharge
1. Determine the destination ZIP code for your shipment.
2. Select the interactive links above for lists of the ZIP codes where the
delivery area surcharge applies. The ZIP codes are listed sequentially .
3. If your destination ZIP code is listed, the delivery area surcharge applies to your shipment. For
surcharge amounts, see the FedEx Ground Economy Fees and Other Shipping Information.
