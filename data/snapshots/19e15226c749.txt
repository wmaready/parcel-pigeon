2027 UPS Small Business Rates Information
Package
Effective December 21, 2026:
- The minimum billable weight for Additional Handling packages will increase from 40 lbs. to 50 lbs.
- A domestic and international Fuel Surcharge of 5% will apply to all Small Business Rates
- The Small Business Rates for UPS® Ground, UPS Air and International services will increase 4.5%.* More detailed rate previews will be available at a later date.
*The impact of these changes on your shipping costs will vary according to your shipping characteristics and the terms of your UPS agreement.
