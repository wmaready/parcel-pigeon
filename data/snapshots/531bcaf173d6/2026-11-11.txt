POSTAL SERVICE
39 CFR Part 501
Authorization To Manufacture and
Distribute Postage Evidencing
Systems
AGENCY: Postal Service.
ACTION: Proposed rule.
SUMMARY: The Postal Service is
amending its Postage Evidencing
Systems regulations to align with
existing practices and operational
realities to better address circumstances
where advances by a resetting company
on any given day exceed the daily
average funds advanced amount
maintained on deposit with the Postal
Service. The regulations currently allow
resetting companies to advance postage
to customers by a process where the
resetting company maintains a daily
average advance balance with the Postal
Service. The current regulation
contemplates that the amount on
deposit will always cover amounts
advanced and does not permit a
resetting company to advance funds to
customers in excess of the amount
maintained on deposit. Operational
realities will create circumstances
where it is necessary to advance funds
in excess of the amount on deposit and
the average daily balance rule represents
a practical and fair method to address
circumstances where excess advances
occur.

(c) If the RC chooses to offer
advancement of funds to customers, the
RC is required to maintain a deposit
with the Postal Service equal to at least
one (1) day’s average funds advanced.
The provider shall transfer to the Postal
Service funds equal to the net amount
advanced for any given day on or before
the morning of the next business day.
The Postal Service shall not be liable for
any payment made by the RC on behalf
of a customer that is not reimbursed by
the customer, since the RC is solely
responsible for the collection of
advances made by the RC.

Approval and Promulgation of Air
Quality Implementation Plans;
Michigan; Withdrawal of the Federal
Implementation Plan for the Detroit
Sulfur Dioxide Area
AGENCY: Environmental Protection
Agency (EPA).
ACTION: Proposed rule.
SUMMARY: The U.S. Environmental
Protection Agency (EPA) is proposing to
withdraw the Federal Implementation
Plan (FIP), promulgated on October 12,
2022 (the ‘‘2022 FIP’’), for attaining the
2010 1-hour primary sulfur dioxide
(SO2) National Ambient Air Quality
Standard (NAAQS) in the Detroit area.
On May 19, 2025, the EPA approved a
revision to Michigan’s State
Implementation Plan (SIP) for attaining
the 2010 1-hour primary SO2 NAAQS in
the Detroit area, rendering the 2022 FIP
duplicative. The EPA is proposing this
action pursuant to Clean Air Act (CAA)
section 110.
DATES: Comments must be received on
or before December 28, 2026.
Virtual Public Hearing. If anyone
contacts the EPA requesting to present
at a virtual public hearing on or before
November 27, 2026, the Agency will
hold a virtual public hearing on
December 14, 2026.
To request to present at a virtual
public hearing, or to pre-register to
attend the hearing, if one is held, please
use the online registration form
available at https://www.epa.gov/mi/
detroit-so2-federal-implementation-plan
or contact Abigail Teener at (312) 353–
7314 or by email at teener.abigail@
epa.gov. The EPA is asking all hearing
attendees to register, even those who do
not intend to present. The last day to
request to present at the hearing will be
November 27, 2026. If no request to
present at the virtual public hearing is

(CT) on November 27, 2026, the EPA

registration until the start of the hearing.
The EPA will announce the status of
the hearing on November 30, 2026 on
the public hearing website at https://
www.epa.gov/mi/detroit-so2-federal-
implementation-plan. Alternatively,
interested parties may contact Abigail
Teener at (312) 353–7314 or by email at
teener.abigail@epa.gov to find out if the
hearing is being held.
