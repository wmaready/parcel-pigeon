Daily Rates
Daily rates will apply if you have a scheduled UPS pickup, or have a UPS contract that provides you with daily rates. Download your zone chart and use the Daily Rate and Service Guide to find your rates.
Retail Rates
Retail rates will apply if you ship from a UPS location, don’t have a payment account or scheduled pickup service with UPS, or you ship infrequently using a payment card. Download your zone chart and use the Retail Rate and Service Guide to find your rates.
Air Freight Rates
Air Freight is for larger shipments that may or may not require a pallet. This can include services like pickup, collection, delivery and customs clearance. Download your zone chart and use the Air Freight Rates Guide to find your rates.
