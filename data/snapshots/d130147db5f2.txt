Fuel surcharges
U.S. fuel surcharges
Fuel surcharge is subject to weekly adjustment.
The fuel surcharge for FedEx Ground®, FedEx Home Delivery®, FedEx International Ground®, and pickup services is based on the weekly published national U.S. on-highway average price for a gallon of diesel fuel.*
The fuel surcharge for all other services is based on the weekly published U.S. Gulf Coast (USGC) spot price for a gallon of kerosene-type jet fuel.*
Effective Nov. 9, 2026
FedEx will adjust the fuel surcharges and update the tables for FedEx domestic services. See the fuel surcharge tables effective Nov. 9, 2026.
*Notwithstanding any information provided herein, the customer agreement is the final authority for all matters related to the customer’s fuel surcharge calculation and the frequency of the calculation adjustment.

The FedEx fuel surcharge for U.S. export freight shipments using FedEx International Express Freight® (IXF) and FedEx International Airport-to-AirportSM (ATA) services is based on chargeable weight. This fuel surcharge rate is subject to change without notice. See the FedEx International Premium® and IXF Terms and Conditions for more details.
Changes to the FedEx fuel surcharge will be applied weekly. The surcharge will be reflected on your FedEx invoice.
- For FedEx Ground, FedEx Home Delivery, and FedEx International Ground, see the on-highway index for the price of a gallon of diesel fuel.
- For other services, see the USGC index for the spot price per gallon of kerosene-type jet fuel.
- The prices in these indexes are published by the U.S. Department of Energy and FedEx is not responsible for the information provided.
For FedEx Ground, FedEx Home Delivery, and FedEx International Ground shipments, the related fuel surcharge is assessed on the net package rate plus the following applicable surcharges: Additional Handling Surcharge, Address Correction, FedEx Ground® Call Tag, Delivery Area Surcharge, FedEx® Delivery Signature Options, FedEx Home Delivery® Convenient Delivery Options, Hazardous Materials, Northern Canada Surcharge, Oversize Charge, Demand — Additional Handling Surcharge, Demand — Ground Unauthorized Package Charge, Demand — Oversize Charge, Demand — Residential Delivery Charge, Ground Unauthorized Package Charge, Residential Delivery Charge, Return Pickup, and any temporary surcharge(s) currently in effect. This list may be amended or augmented without notice.
For other FedEx package and express freight shipments, excluding shipments where a weight-based calculation is used, the related fuel surcharge is assessed on the net package rate plus the following applicable surcharges: Accessible Dangerous Goods, Additional Handling Surcharge, Demand – Additional Handling Surcharge, Additional Handling Surcharge – Non-stackable, Address Correction, Broker Select Option Fee, Delivery Area Surcharge, Delivery Reattempt Charge, Demand Surcharge, Extended Service Area Delivery, Dry Ice, Extended Service Area Pickup, FedEx® Authenticated Delivery, FedEx® Delivery Signature Options, FedEx ExpressTag®, Hazardous Materials, Inaccessible Dangerous Goods, International Out-of-Delivery-Area Surcharge, International Out-of-Pickup-Area Surcharge, Metro Service Area Delivery, Metro Service Area Pickup, Oversize Charge, Demand — Oversize Charge, Pickup Charge (FedEx International Premium), Reroute of Shipment, Residential Delivery Charge, Demand — Residential Delivery Charge, Residential Pickup Charge, Return Pickup, Saturday Delivery, Saturday Pickup, Unauthorized Charge, and any temporary surcharge(s) currently in effect. This list may be amended or augmented without notice.
For FedEx express freight shipments where a weight-based calculation is used, the related fuel surcharge is assessed on the billable weight of the shipment. Billable weight may include additional deficit weight when applicable.
