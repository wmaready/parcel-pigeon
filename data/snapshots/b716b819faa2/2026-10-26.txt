UPS Demand Surcharges1
One or more Demand Surcharges will apply to packages shipped during the specified Demand Periods (inclusive of any applicable
starting and ending dates) for the origins, destinations, and service levels and in the amounts set forth below. Demand Surcharges
apply in addition to all other applicable Charges.
Demand Surcharges are subject to change and Demand Periods may be extended or otherwise changed. Shippers should continue to check
ups.com/peaksurcharge for updates prior to tendering shipments.

Surcharge will apply to all packages that are Over Maximum Limits for all U.S. domestic shipments. The Demand Surcharges will apply in the
amounts set forth below.
