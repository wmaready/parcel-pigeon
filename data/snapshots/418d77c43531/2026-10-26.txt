UPS Ground Domestic and UPS Ground
Saver® (Formerly Known as UPS
SurePost®) Fuel Surcharge
UPS uses an index-based surcharge that is
adjusted weekly. Changes to the surcharge will be
effective on Monday of each week. The surcharge
will be based on the National U.S. Average On-
Highway Diesel Fuel Price as most recently
released by the U.S. Energy Information
Administration (EIA) rounded to the nearest cent.
For example, the surcharge for the week of March
2, 2026 is based on the National U.S. Average On-
Highway Diesel Fuel Price released by the EIA the
week of February 23, 2026.
UPS Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge
UPS uses an index-based surcharge that is
adjusted weekly. Changes to the surcharge will be
effective on Monday of each week. The surcharge
will be based on U.S. Gulf Coast (USGC) price for
kerosene-type jet fuel as most recently released
by the U.S. Energy Information Administration
(EIA) rounded to the nearest cent. For example,
the surcharge for the week of March 2, 2026 is
based on the U.S. Gulf Coast (USGC) price for
kerosene-type jet fuel released by the EIA the
week of February 23, 2026.

Services Affected
The U.S. Ground Domestic and UPS Ground Saver® Fuel Surcharge will apply to charges for the following services:
UPS Ground®, UPS® Ground with Freight Pricing, UPS Hundredweight Ground Service, UPS Ground Saver® and
UPS® Basic.
The U.S. Ground Domestic and UPS Ground Saver Fuel Surcharge does not apply to UPS Ground packages originating
in the 48 contiguous United States when subject to Retail rates.
The Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge will apply to charges for the following services:
UPS Next Day Air® Early, UPS Next Day Air®, UPS Next Day Air Saver®, UPS 2nd Day Air A.M.®, UPS
2nd Day Air®, UPS 3 Day Select®, and UPS Hundredweight Air services.
Fuel surcharges apply to all domestic and international transportation charges and certain other accessorial charges,
value added charges, surcharges and other charges. For a complete list of accessorial charges, value added charges,
surcharges and other charges to which the Fuel Surcharge applies, review
https://www.ups.com/assets/resources/webcontent/en_US/fsc-applicable-charges.pdf
