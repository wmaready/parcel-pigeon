Demand Surcharge for U.S. international services
Updated Aug. 18, 2026
Non-standard and oversized items require additional labor and equipment to sort, handle, and deliver safely. As
FedEx prepares for high demand during the holiday season, we are implementing the following fees to offset the
cost of these items during this time of the year.

the Unauthorized Charge will be considered and referred to as a Non-standard shipment.
2 See the FedEx Service Guide for the criteria and characteristics of these surcharges.
3 Excludes FedEx International Ground® shipments.
