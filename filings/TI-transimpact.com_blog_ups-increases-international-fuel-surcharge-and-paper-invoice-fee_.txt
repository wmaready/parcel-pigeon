UPS Announces Increases to International Fuel Surcharge and Invoice Fee

 UPS Announces Increases to International Fuel Surcharge and Invoice Fee

 By: 
 Brian Byrd

 May 12, 2025

 Summarize this article using AI

One week after UPS announced an increase to its Additional Handling Surcharge (AHS), Large Package Surcharge (LPS), and zone change, the parcel carrier is updating its International Fuel Surcharge table and implementing a new International Paper Invoice Fee.

Details of the new costs include:

International FSC percentages will increase by 1% on May 12. More information is available on the UPS website (click here.)
International paper invoice fee of $12 will begin on May 26. More information is available on the UPS website (click here.)

To understand how the rate changes will impact your small parcel shipping costs, email sales-info@transimpact.com for a no-obligation analysis of your parcel spend and ideas on how you can mitigate the impact on your shipping budget.
