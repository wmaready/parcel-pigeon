 FedEx Increases Domestic and International Fuel Surcharges

 By: 
 Brian Byrd

 June 3, 2025

 Summarize this article using AI

FedEx has announced a domestic and international fuel surcharge increase that will be effective beginning June 9. (click here for the updated fuel table)

The impact on customers is a 2% increase over the current surcharge amount. The increase follows recent UPS fuel surcharge increases of 1% for both domestic and international fuel that went into effect in May.

Contact sales-info@transimpact.com for a no-obligation analysis of how the increases will impact your shipping costs as the market heads into peak shipping season. We can also share new ideas on ways you can prepare your parcel supply chain for the busiest time of year for many companies.
