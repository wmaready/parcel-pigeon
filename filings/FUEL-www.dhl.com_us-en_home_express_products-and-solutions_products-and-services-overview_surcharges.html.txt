 Remote Area

Applies when collecting from or delivering to locations outside standard DHL Express service areas. 

Surcharge | Description | How We Charge | Cost

Remote Area Delivery | When shipping to a remote area DHL Express applies a surcharge per shipment when the pick up or delivery destination location is remote. Remote is defined as distant islands and highlands, a postal code, suburb or town that is difficult, inaccessible or infrequently served. | Per Shipment | 50.00 USD or 0.50 USD per lb. (or per ½ kg), whichever is higher

 Security

Applies to shipments to or from destinations classified as elevated risk or subject to trade restrictions. 

Surcharge | Description | How We Charge | Cost

Elevated Risk | Countries where DHL Express operates at elevated risk. A surcharge is applied when shipping to or importing from a destination country, DHL Express is operating at elevated risk due to continuous state of war, civil unrest, or continuous threats from terrorism. The list of destination countries in scope is published online as part of the respective surcharge table. This charge may apply in addition to either Restricted Destination or Exporter Validation. Countries affected (subject to change): Afghanistan, Burkina Faso, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Haiti, Iraq, Lebanon, Libya, Mali, Somalia Mogadishu, Sudan, Syria, Ukraine, Venezuela, and Yemen. | Per Shipment | 30.00 USD

Restricted Destination | Shipping in compliance with trade restrictions A surcharge applies when shipping to or importing from a destination country that is subject to specific trade restrictions imposed by the UN Security Council or by national legislation such as the European Union or the US Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC). Countries affected (subject to change): Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Iran, Iraq, North Korea, Libya, Somalia Mogadishu and Yemen Countries subject to national legislation (subject to change): Afghanistan, Belarus, Cuba, Lebanon, Myanmar, Russia | Per Shipment | 30.00 USD

 Data Correction

Applies when shipment or billing information requires correction after submission, including address updates. 

Surcharge | Description | How We Charge | Cost

Address Correction | Our priority is to get your shipment in the right hands. A surcharge is applied per shipment when the destination address provided by the shipper is incomplete, outdated or incorrect and DHL Express is subsequently unable to deliver the shipment. DHL Express searches and determines the correct address to complete the delivery. | Per Shipment | 25.50 USD

Data Entry | Manual Entry Data by DHL Express A surcharge applies if the shipping label is not correctly completed or has not been produced electronically according to DHL Express standards and therefore requires manual data entry by DHL Express. | Per Shipment | 6.50 USD

 Additional Handling

Applies to shipments that require special handling due to size, weight, packaging, or stacking limitations. 

Surcharge | Description | How We Charge | Cost

Overweight Piece | Shipment pieces exceeding 70 kg A fixed surcharge is applied to every piece, including a pallet, that exceeds a scale or volumetric weight of 150 lbs. for shipments originating in the U.S. and U.S. Territories (70 kg. for shipments originating outside of the U.S. and U.S. Territories). | Per Piece | 100.00 USD

Non-Stackable Pallet | When shipping non-stackable pallets This fixed surcharge is applied to every pallet within a shipment that cannot be stacked, either on request by the shipper, or because of its shape, content or packaging material. Does not apply to pallets below 25 kg. | Per Pallet | 355.00 USD

Oversize Piece | A fixed surcharge is applied to every piece, including a pallet, if its longest side exceeds 100 cm (40 in.) or its second longest side exceeds 80 cm (32 in.) for shipments originating in the U.S. and U.S. Territories (>100 cm (40 in.) longest side / >80 cm (32 in.) second longest side) for shipments originating outside of the U.S. and U.S. Territories. Does not apply to pieces already subject to Overweight Piece surcharge. | Per Piece | 30.00 USD

Non-Conveyable Piece | Shipment pieces between 25 kg - 70 kg / 56 lb - 150 lbA fixed surcharge is applied to every piece within a shipment that meets one or more of the following criteria:Has an actual weight between 25 kg and 70 kg / 56 lbs. and 150 lbs. Is not fully enclosed in an outer shipping containerIs packaged in materials other than corrugated cardboard, such as metal, wood, leather, hard or soft plastic, or expanded polystyrene foamIs wrapped in shrink wrap or stretch wrapHas a round or cylindrical shape, such as buckets, cans, drums, tires, or barrelsIncludes wheels, handles, or strapsHas the potential to become entangled with or cause damage to other packages or the handling systemsThe surcharge does not apply to pieces already subject to Overweight Piece or Oversize Piece surcharge. | Per Piece | 30.00 USD

 More About Domestic and International Time Definite services

The fuel surcharge for all services will be based on the daily average spot prices for US Gulf Coast (USGC) kerosene-type jet fuel, as reported by the U.S. Department of Energy between third week release of prior month to third week release of actual month.

For example, the daily average price for fuel from 18th of April to 15th of May is used to determine the applicable surcharge in June.

The fuel surcharge applies to the transportation charges and to the following services and surcharges (where applicable):

Dedicated DeliveryDedicated PickupDemand SurchargeElevated RiskEmergency SituationNon-Conveyable PieceNon-Stackable PalletOversize PieceOverweight PieceRemote Area DeliveryRemote Area PickupResidential AddressSaturday DeliverySaturday Pickup

DHL Express reserves the right to change the fuel surcharge index and table with or without notice. Both the amount and duration of the surcharge will be determined at DHL Express' sole discretion.
