UPS Announces Significant Surcharge Updates and Rate Increases

 UPS Announces Significant Surcharge Updates and Rate Increases

 By: 
 Brian Byrd

 May 22, 2025

 Summarize this article using AI

UPS recently announced several surcharge updates worth noting.

Here is a link to the latest information on the UPS website: Shipping Costs and Rates | UPS – United States

Domestic Large Package Surcharge and Additional Handling Charge

Effective Date: August 17, 2025

Domestic Large Package Surcharge (LPS):

 LPS will no longer be applied to Domestic Packages based on length plus girth. 
 Domestic Packages with a cubic size greater than 17,280 cubic inches will be subject to LPS (cubic size = length x width x height). 
 Domestic Packages that weigh over 110 lbs will be subject LPS (this is new for the application of LPS). 
 Other factors currently used to determine LPS applicability will remain unchanged (length exceeding 96 inches). 

Surcharge Amounts:

Commercial packages: Weight, Cubic Size, Length

 Zone 2: $205.00 
 Zone 3-4: $225.00 
 Zone 5-6: $250.00 
 Zone 7+: $260.00 

Residential packages: Weight, Cubic Size, Length

 Zone 2: $240.00 
 Zone 3-4: $260.00 
 Zone 5-6: $297.50 
 Zone 7+: $305.00 

Domestic Additional Handling Charge (AHC):

 AHC will no longer be applied to Domestic Packages based on length plus girth. 
 Domestic Packages with a cubic size greater than 8,640 cubic inches will be subject to AHC (cubic size = length x width x height). 
 Other factors currently used to determine AHC applicability will remain unchanged (weight exceeding 50 lbs, length of longest side exceeding 48 inches, width of second longest side exceeding 30 inches, certain packaging). 

Surcharge Amounts:

Domestic (Zone 2) (Zone 3-4) (Zone 5-6) (Zone 7+)
 Weight ($43.50) ($47.50) ($52.75) ($55.00)
 Cubic Size ($28.00) ($31.00) ($36.00) ($38.00)
 Length ($28.00) ($31.00) ($36.00) ($38.00)
 Width ($28.00) ($31.00) ($36.00) ($38.00)
 Packaging ($25.00) ($29.00) ($31.00) ($31.50)

Domestic Fuel Surcharge

Effective May 26, 2025

The U.S. Ground Domestic and the UPS Ground Saver® Fuel Surcharge and the U.S. Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge will change. Details on these changes can be found using this link: Read More About the Rate Change

This update increases Domestic Air, Ground, and Ground Saver fuel surcharge percentages by 1%. The last domestic FSC increase by UPS was +.5% in early March.

Delivery Area Surcharges

Effective Date: June 1, 2025

The list of ZIP codes to which Area Surcharges apply will change. The current list of Area Surcharge ZIP codes can be found here.
