 FedEx Implements New Demand Surcharge for Shipments to Canada

 By: 
 Brian Byrd

 May 29, 2025

 Summarize this article using AI

FedEx has announced a new Demand Surcharge Fee on exports to Canada, effective June 2:

$0.49 per lb. for Export Parcel and Freight to Canada

$0.49 per package for FedEx International Ground to Canada.

Note that this new FedEx fee is similar to the UPS Surge Fee on shipments to Canada that went into effect May 18.

There was no end date announced for either fee.

Contact sales-info@transimpact.com with any questions or for a no-obligation analysis of how the new fees will impact your shipping costs.
