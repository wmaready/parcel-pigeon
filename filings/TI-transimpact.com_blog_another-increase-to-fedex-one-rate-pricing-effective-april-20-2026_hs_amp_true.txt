 Another increase to FedEx One Rate pricing - effective April 20, 2026

 Written by Brian Byrd | Apr 15, 2026 12:39:56 PM 

FedEx has officially announced another increase to its FedEx One Rate®️pricing, set to take effect on April 20, 2026.

What Is FedEx One Rate?

FedEx One Rate is a flat-rate shipping service for U.S. domestic shipments using FedEx-supplied packaging. It's popular with businesses because shippers get waived common surcharges like fuel, residential, and delivery area fees are waived. This keeps pricing simple and predictable compared to traditional parcel pricing.

What's Changing?

FedEx has announced that FedEx One Rate pricing will increase effective April 20, 2026. This follows a series of rate adjustments the carrier has made throughout 2026, including an earlier update in February that added pricing for the new FedEx Extra Small Box.
