 TransImpact’s Expert Predictions for the 2025 FedEx and UPS General Rate Increases (GRI)

 By: 
 Brian Byrd

 September 9, 2024

 Summarize this article using AI

It’s almost that time of year. The upcoming GRI announcements are the biggest news of the season for FedEx and UPS customers because the annual increases have an immense impact on most companies’ shipping costs and profitability.

As we do each year, TransImpact is helping companies get a head start preparing for what’s to come with our expert predictions in our GRI Prediction Report.

And… you’ll want to read it because of the many mixed signals appearing in the marketplace right now that make understanding the latest rate trends extra complicated.

In our report, you’ll get predictions on the details of the upcoming GRIs down to the surcharge level and guidance on preparing your shipping operation for 2025. It also includes twelve detailed illustrations and many more expert insights. Don’t forget to keep reading until the end to find some BOLD PREDICTIONS that may shake up parcel shipping even more in 2025.
