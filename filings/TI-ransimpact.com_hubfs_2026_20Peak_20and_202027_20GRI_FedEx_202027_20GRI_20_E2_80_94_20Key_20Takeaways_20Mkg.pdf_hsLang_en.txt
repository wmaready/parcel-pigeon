Priority Overnight cools while 2 Day
holds steady
Priority Overnight has softened year over
year, down from roughly 6.8% in 2026 to
6.53% in 2027. 2 Day, meanwhile, has barely
moved: 6.20% in 2026 to 6.35% in 2027.
Express Saver may be the GRI's ballast
Don't be alarmed by Express Saver's low
3.09% — it's likely one of the levers FedEx
uses to land near its published 5.9% "overall"
average while other services run hotter. It ran
a similarly muted 3.8% in 2026, and this year
it's even lower.
Ground: steady, not standout
Ground & Home Delivery sits near the middle
at 6.10% — but that average hides a real
story underneath. Its true driver is weight,
not size (see below).

Three different shapes, one takeaway — weight tiers barely separate for Express , but zones do. Priority Overnight stays nearly flat
everywhere except Zone 2, where the lightest tier (1–10 lbs) spikes to 6.89% — its single highest point. 2 Day shows a wide split in Zone 2 (tiers
fan from 6.38% down to 5.01%) before converging into a tight band from Zone 3 on. Standard Overnight is the flattest of the three almost
everywhere (~4.9%) — except Zone 3, which jumps to roughly 6.5–6.6% across all weight tiers alike, the one zone where it actually runs hotter
than the published 5.9% GRI.

Small parcels aren't getting a pass on Ground
It's tempting to assume the increase scales with box size — it
doesn't. The 1–5 lb tier takes the steepest average hit (6.49%),
ahead of even the 71+ lb tier (6.21%), while the 21–50 lb middle
range is where FedEx is comparatively gentle (as low as 5.50%). See
the full breakdown above.
Standard Overnight's Zone 3 doesn't match its
own average
Standard Overnight runs a mild ~4.9–5.0% almost everywhere —
except Zone 3, which jumps to 6.6%. If a customer ships meaningful
Zone 3 Standard Overnight volume, their real exposure is well above
the headline 5.2% service average.

AHS and DAS are the categories to watch
Additional Handling (AHS) surcharges average +7.4% across all 12
dimension/weight/package tiers — the single hottest category in
the file. Delivery Area (DAS) isn't far behind at +7.0%, and its
"extended" zones (9.1%, 8.1%) are rising nearly 50% faster than its
standard zones (6.1%, 5.6%).
Residential surcharges are climbing too
The Ground residential surcharge rises from $6.45 to $6.90 —
+6.98% — while the Express version goes from $6.95 to $7.35,
+5.76% . Both land in the middle of the accessorial pack, but they
hit nearly every residential delivery, so the dollars add up fast.
