 Small Parcel Carrier Pricing Updates 

With pricing elements changing throughout the year, it’s harder than ever to keep up with how UPS and FedEx rate increases and surcharges impact your business. Off-cycle adjustments are becoming the norm — not the exception. TransImpact highlights the key 2025 updates you need to know.

2026

Gold = UPS

Purple = Fedex

January 14

UPS announced a temporary Surge Fee on all U.S. export shipments (except those to Israel) effective January 18, 2026, through February 14, 2026.

 Read More >>

February 18

Effective March 2, 2026, UPS is increasing the international ground fuel surcharge by 1 percentage point, resulting in higher cross-border shipping costs that will continue to fluctuate weekly with diesel prices. 

 Read More >>

March 5

 FedEx introduced a new international demand surcharge for shipments moving between the U.S. and Zone K (Middle East). The surcharge took effect March 5, 2026 and will remain in place until further notice. 

 Read More >>

March 9

 UPS announced a 1% increase to its domestic fuel surcharge index, effective March 9, 2026. The change applies to U.S. Ground Domestic, Ground Saver, and Domestic Air services, and comes just two months after the last domestic fuel surcharge increase. 

 Read More >>

April 19

 UPS rolled out a broad "Surge Emergency Fee" on April 19, applying a $0.23 per-pound charge to shipments between the United States and most international destinations. 

 Read More >>

April 20

 FedEx has announced that FedEx One Rate pricing will increase effective April 20, 2026. This follows a series of rate adjustments the carrier has made throughout 2026, including an earlier update in February that added pricing for the new FedEx Extra Small Box. 

 Read More >>

May 13

 FedEx’s board of directors approved the spinoff of its less-than-truckload business, FedEx Freight. 

 Read More >>

May 31

UPS has increased Surge Emergency Fees for accessorials and international fuel surcharge tables for both ground and air services.

 Read More >>

June 22

FedEx is consolidating its previously separate Export and Import fuel surcharge tables into a single unified table, resulting in a 3.5% increase in export shipments and a 0.75% decrease in import shipments.

 Read More >>

July 23

FedEx releases 2026-27 Peak Season surcharges. TransImpact experts prepared a Pricing Breakdown for you. 

 Read More >>

July 20

 Added 28 standard contiguous U.S., 10 extended, and 74 remote ZIP codes for both Delivery Area Surcharges (DAS) and Pickup Area Surcharges (PAS).

 Read More >>

July 30

Disbursement Fee, increase from the greater of $15 or 2% of duty, tax, or merchandise fee processing charges to the greater of $17.50 or 2.5% of duty, tax, or merchandise fee processing charges 

 Read More >>

August 1

 UPS is increasing export fuel 1% 

 Read More >>

August 3

 US Inbound Processing Fee renamed Inbound Processing Fee, fee extended to all shipments destined for EU member states. The fee will not apply to shipments from one EU member state to another. 

 Read More >>

August 10

 UPS ground and ground saver 0.5%-1% increase on lower bands & lower bands expanded, last FSC update for UPS was 4.13.26 

 Read More >>

August 27

UPS has released its 2026–27 peak demand surcharges. The flat Ground fee rose 25 percent, and the Air fee rose 22 percent. Meanwhile, additional handling rose 10.2 percent, the Large Package Surcharge 9.8 percent, and Over Maximum Limits are up 9.3 percent.

 Read More >>

September 18

FedEx has announced an average 5.9% General Rate Increase (GRI), effective Jan. 4, 2027.

 Read More >>

2025

Gold = UPS

Purple = Fedex

January 14

Domestic Express & Ground Fuel Surcharge percentages increased by 1% for Domestic Express & 1.75% for Ground and Home Delivery.

 Read More >>

January 14

Effective Feb. 10 — Ground Economy DAS will increase from $4.00 to $6.20 & Extended DAS will rise from $5.35 to $8.30 (55% increase, in line with UPS SurePost DAS).

 Read More >>

February 24

Applicable zone pricing will change for certain origin/destination ZIP code pairs.

 Read More >>

March 6

Fuel surcharge percentages will increase by 0.5% for Domestic Air & Domestic Ground/SurePost.

 Read More >>

March 6

A print invoice fee was introduced ($5 per invoice copy). Check/wire fee set at $25 per payment, and the late payment fee rose from 8% to 9.9%.

 Read More >>

March 6

Payment Processing Fee announced — 2% of all invoice charges. Credit card surcharge no longer applies.

 Read More >>

April 23

Mail Innovations rates increased, with some seeing a 40% increase.

 Read More >>

April 23

A $1.75 Delivery Area Surcharge was added for extended area ZIP codes. A 1.5% late payment fee now applies for Mail Innovations.

 Read More >>

May 21

U.S. Ground Domestic, UPS Ground Saver Fuel Surcharge, & U.S. Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge tables will change.

 Read More >>

May 21

UPS will update its list of ZIP codes for DAS surcharges.

 Read More >>

May 21

UPS Domestic LPS & AHS pricing will change across all zones.

 Read More >>

May 29

UPS implemented a surge fee on shipments from the U.S. to Canada ($1.25/lb for Worldwide Economy and $0.49 for other export services). No end date has been announced.

 Read More >>

May 29

FedEx announced demand surcharges on shipments to Canada.

 Read More >>

June 3

FedEx announced a domestic & international fuel surcharge, a 2% increase over the previous surcharge amount.

 Read More >>

June 19

UPS Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge increased by 0.75%.

 Read More >>

July 8

FedEx announced its Peak Season Surcharges for 2025. 

 Read More >>

July 14

FedEx international package and express freight shipments that exceed the maximum allowed dimensions and weight will incur the applicable Unauthorized Package or Unauthorized Freight Charge.

 Read More >>

July 14

FedEx AHS for zones 5-6 increased — dimensions from $34 to $36, weight from $50.50 to $52.75, and packaging from $30.50 to $31.

 Read More >>

July 14

FedEx Oversize: surcharge increased: zone 2 from $205 to $240, zones 3-4 from $225 to $260, zones 5-6 from $240 to $297.50, zones 7+ from $260 to $305. Home Delivery zones 5-6 will increase from $290 to $297.50.

 Read More >>

July 14

FedEx Ground Unauthorized package increased from $1,325 to $1,775 per package (UPS charge is $1,775 for Over Maximum Limits)

 Read More >>

July 14

Additional Handling Surcharge for international packages: The Weight threshold decreased from 70 lbs. to 55 lbs.

 Read More >>

July 14

FedEx One Rate pricing will change for Express Saver and 2-Day services.

 Read More >>

July 18

FedEx will round every fraction of an inch/centimeter up to the next-higher unit. Inbound Processing Fee will increase for U.S. import shipments associated with customs clearance.

 Read More >>

August 21

UPS implemented an international processing fee of $2.50 per import shipment.

 Read More >>

August 25

UPS implemented an international processing fee of $2.50 per import shipment.

 Read More >>

August 28

UPS announced its 2025 Peak Season Surcharges.

 Read More >>

September 5

International Connect Plus will be assessed a U.S. Inbound Processing Fee on U.S. import shipments, with two additional Clearance Entry Fee rate tiers added for International Ground® Canada-to-U.S. shipments.

 Read More >>

September 12

FedEx announced its 2026 GRI.

 Read More >>

September 15

FedEx announced its international Peak Season surcharges.

 Read More >>

September 15

UPS announced its international Peak Season surcharges.

 Read More >>

October 31

UPS announces its 2026 GRI.

 Read More >>

November 20

UPS announced an update to International Air-Export and International Air-Import fuel surcharges.

 Read More >>

November 20

Effective January 5, 2026: UPS will increase its Domestic Ground Fuel Surcharge to 21.75% (from 20.75%) and Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge to 21% (from 20%), a +1% adjustment across domestic services.

December 1

FedEx adjusted domestic fuel indexes effective Dec. 1, 2025, driving a 1.5%+ increase in fuel surcharges across services.

FedEx suspends Money-Back Guarantee amid MD-11 inspections, impacting service commitments through peak and into 2026.
