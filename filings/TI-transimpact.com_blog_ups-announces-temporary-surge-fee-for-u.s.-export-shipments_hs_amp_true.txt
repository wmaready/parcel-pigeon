 UPS Announces Temporary Surge Fee for U.S. Export Shipments

 Written by Brian Byrd | Jan 14, 2026 4:50:25 PM 

UPS has announced a temporary Surge Fee that will apply to U.S. export shipments to all destinations (except Israel).

Key details:

Effective date: January 18, 2026

End date: February 14, 2026

Applies to: All exports from the U.S. to all destinations (excluding Israel)

UPS notes that Surge Fees are subject to change, and UPS demand/surge fees apply in addition to all other applicable charges. Shippers should monitor invoice activity closely throughout the effective period to understand the full cost impact.
