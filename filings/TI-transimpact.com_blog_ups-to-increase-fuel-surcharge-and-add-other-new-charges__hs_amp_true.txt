 UPS to Increase Fuel Surcharge and Add Other New Charges - TransImpact

 Written by Brian Byrd | Mar 7, 2025 5:00:00 AM 

UPS has recently announced the implementation of several new fees and an increase to fuel percentages on some services. Here is a list:

Fuel Surcharges: Effective March 10, 2025

 Fuel surcharge percentages will increase by .5% for Domestic Air and Domestic Ground/SurePost. 
 Domestic Ground/SurePost Fuel Surcharges will now apply to weekly scheduled pickup fees. 

Other Fees: Effective March 31, 2025

 Print Invoice Fee: $5 per invoice copy 
 Check Fee and Wire Fee: $25 per payment (does not apply to ACH payments) 
 Late payment fee to increase from 8% to 9.9% 

Credit Card Fees: Effective May 18, 2025

 Payment Processing Fee: a fee of 2% of all other invoice charges added 
 Credit Card Surcharge: no longer applicable 

Contact TransImpact with any questions about these changes and how your small parcel shipping costs may be impacted.
