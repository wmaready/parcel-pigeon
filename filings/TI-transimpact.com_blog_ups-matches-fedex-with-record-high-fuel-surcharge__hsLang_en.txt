UPS Matches FedEx with Record-Breaking Fuel Surcharge Hike

 UPS Matches FedEx with Record-Breaking Fuel Surcharge Hike

 By: 
 Brian Byrd

 June 20, 2025

 Summarize this article using AI

UPS announced a domestic fuel surcharge increase effective June 23, following the recent FedEx update from earlier in June. With this increase, both the UPS and FedEx Ground Fuel Surcharge will be 20%, a record high for both carriers. The previous peak was by FedEx, which reached 19.50% during mid-2022.

The Domestic Air fuel Surcharge will increase 0.75% , matching FDX Express at 18.75%.
