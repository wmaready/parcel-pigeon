Update to U.S. Fuel Surcharges

Domestic Fuel Surcharge

Effective November 2, 2026, the U.S. Ground Domestic and the UPS Ground Saver® Fuel Surcharge and the U.S. Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge will change. Details on these changes can be found using the link below:

Read More About the Rate Change

The U.S. Ground Domestic and UPS Ground Saver® Fuel Surcharge will continue to be based on the National U.S. Average On-Highway Diesel Fuel Price and adjusted weekly.

The U.S. Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge will continue to be based on the National Average U.S. Gulf Coast Jet Fuel Price and adjusted weekly.

Changes to the U.S. Ground Domestic and UPS Ground Saver® Fuel Surcharge and the U.S. Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge will be reflected on our Fuel Surcharge website beginning October 29, 2026.

Billing

If you have a UPS account number, the Fuel Surcharge will be included on your weekly bill. If you are an occasional UPS customer and do not receive a weekly bill, a Fuel Surcharge will be included in your charges.

U.S. Ground Domestic and UPS Ground Saver® (Formerly Known as UPS SurePost®) Fuel Surcharge

UPS uses an index-based surcharge that is adjusted weekly. Changes to the surcharge will be effective on Monday of each week. The surcharge will be based on the National U.S. Average On-Highway Diesel Fuel Price as most recently released by the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) rounded to the nearest cent. For example, the surcharge for the week of March 21, 2022 is based on the National U.S. Average On-Highway Diesel Fuel Price released by the EIA the week of March 14, 2022.

The U.S. Ground Domestic Fuel Surcharge and Ground Fuel Surcharge for UPS Ground Saver® (Formerly Known as UPS SurePost®) will be set in relation to the following table:

National U.S. Average On-highway Diesel Fuel Price (USD/Gallon)

USDiesel

  • At Least
  • But Less Than
  • Surcharge

Fuel Surcharge percentages and thresholds are subject to change without prior notice. If the Fuel Surcharge falls outside the published range: (i) for fuel prices above $4.99, the range will extend in $0.09 increments and the Fuel Surcharge will adjust in 0.25% increments; and (ii) for fuel prices below $4.99 the range will extend in $0.27 increments and the Fuel Surcharge will adjust in 0.25% increments.

For the released diesel fuel pricing information, please see the U.S. EIA's National U.S. Average On Highway Diesel Fuel Price. If the diesel fuel pricing information released by the EIA is delayed, UPS may use released diesel fuel pricing information from an earlier week.

Services Affected

The U.S. Ground Domestic and UPS Ground Saver® (Formerly Known as UPS SurePost®) Fuel Surcharge will apply to charges for the following services:

UPS Ground®, UPS® Ground with Freight Pricing, UPS Hundredweight Ground Service, UPS Ground Saver® (Formerly Known as UPS SurePost®) and UPS® Basic.

The U.S. Ground Domestic Fuel Surcharge does not apply to UPS Ground packages originating in the 48 contiguous United States when subject to Retail rates.

The Domestic Air Fuel Surcharge will apply to charges for the following services:

UPS Next Day Air® Early, UPS Next Day Air®, UPS Next Day Air Saver®, UPS 2nd Day Air A.M.®, UPS 2nd Day Air®, UPS 3 Day Select®, and UPS Hundredweight Air services.

The International Air-Export and International Air-Import Fuel Surcharge will apply to charges for the following services:

UPS 3 Day Select® from Canada, UPS Worldwide Express Plus®, UPS Worldwide Express NA1®, UPS Worldwide Express®, UPS Worldwide Express Freight® Midday, UPS Worldwide Express Freight®, UPS Worldwide Saver®, UPS Worldwide Expedited®, and UPS Express Critical®.

The International Ground Export Import Fuel Surcharge will apply to charges for the following services:

UPS® Standard to/from Canada and UPS® Standard to/from Mexico.

Fuel surcharges apply to all domestic and international transportation charges and certain other accessorial, value-added and other charges. For a list of accessorial, value-added and other charges subject to the Fuel Surcharge, review  Accessorial FSC List.
